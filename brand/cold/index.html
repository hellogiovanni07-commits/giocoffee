Line essentials

GioCold is not a new company. It's the cold bar inside every GioCoffee, with its own look so the cold menu stands out on a hot Manila afternoon.

Our promise

Cold coffee takes 20 hours. Yours still has your name on it.

Every batch of cold brew starts the day before you drink it: Benguet Medium, coarse ground, steeped cold for 20 hours, then filtered twice. We write the batch number and your name on every iced cup, so you know exactly when your coffee started. The drip tower and the nitro taps live at GioCoffee Reserve, 5th Ave BGC.

Do

  • Say the steep time ("20 hours")
  • Give the batch number on boards and cups
  • Use the website's sizes: Medio, Grande, Gigante
  • Write "less ice" and "no ice" as plain options
  • Credit the beans ("Benguet Medium")

Don't

  • Use "chill", "brrr" or "ice ice baby" puns
  • Call iced coffee "cold brew"; they are different
  • Write prices with decimals
  • Say "refreshing" or "energising"; show it instead
  • Promise caffeine effects

Logo

The same round badge as the parent, gone cold: Night Green, a dotted ring of condensation, a clear iced cup with a foam cap and two cubes. The gold straw rises out of the cup and bends into a G, where the parent's steam used to curl.

Construction how the mark is built

  1. BadgeThe parent's round door, in Night Green: the colour of the tap wall after dark.
  2. Drop ringWhere the parent has a solid saucer rim, we have dots: condensation on a cold cup.
  3. Clear cupWhite outline, see-through body. A cold cup is clear so you see what's inside.
  4. Foam, brew, iceA Cold Foam cap, the brew, two cubes and a white label for the name.
  5. Straw GOne gold stroke from the bottom of the cup, through the lid, bent into a G.

Never redraw the mark. Use the files in chapter 17.

Gradients

Gradients only behind products or on big bands. Never behind body text.

The never-list

  • No icy or electric blue: cold here means green-grey, like a wet glass
  • No pure black: the darkest colour is Steep, a coffee brown
  • No white snowflakes, frost borders or icicles
  • Gold stays thin: a straw, a rule, an arc, a star

Drink layer colours cups, illustrations and the 3D models

The same layers as the website's 3D cups, top to bottom. Use them only to draw the drinks, never as brand colours.

16

Motion

Cold moves like ice and nitro: a quick drop, a small settle, a slow cascade. The parent's calm ease stays; the drop gets a tiny overshoot, never a bounce. Demos play only when you press Play.

Ice drop

A cube falls, dips, settles; the drink rises a little. 0.9 s. Adding a cold drink to the order.

Nitro cascade

Light lines fall, the body darkens, the foam head forms. 2.4 s. Nitro item sheet, the tap story.

Steep clock

The gold arc draws from midnight to the steep time. 1.6 s. Batch board on screens, the app's cold tab.

Straw G

The straw draws up out of the cup and bends into the G, then the cubes appear. 1.2 s. App launch, cold page loads.

Toolkit & checklist

Download the files, then go through every material. "Design ready" means the design is final in this kit; a printer still makes the print file.

Logo and artwork files (SVG)

Text in the files uses Plus Jakarta Sans and Caveat. Install the fonts, or convert text to outlines before printing.

Design tokens & details

Before you print

  • PDF, CMYK, 300 dpi, 3 mm bleed
  • Night Green as a Pantone spot on labels and cans; proof it on frost film
  • Print the name label on matte film that takes marker when wet
  • Text at least 3 mm inside the trim
  • Replace every placeholder first (use "Highlight placeholders")
