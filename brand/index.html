Brand essentials

One idea carries the whole brand: a drink made for one person, with their name on it. If a design or a sentence doesn't serve that, cut it.

The promise, as the site says it

Every drink is made to order and every cup carries your name, written by your barista.

GioCoffee is handcrafted coffee from the Philippine highlands and three farms abroad, roasted in Taguig. Four stores, two in BGC and two in Makati. Uptown never closes; the 5th Ave Reserve has a slow bar, a drive-thru and seats under the acacias.

Three words: warm, crafted, neighbourly. A good cup made with care by people who remember you, with a little Manila humour ("Pasko na, basically").

Brand pillars what every piece of work should prove

1Your name, by hand

The barista writes it on the cup in marker. It's on the member card, the order label, the pick-up shelf and the "see you" at the end of every order.

2Made to order, your way

Size, milk, shots, syrup: change anything and the bar gets exactly that. Calories and caffeine follow the size you pick.

3Philippine beans first

Benguet and Sagada arabica next to Sumatra, Yirgacheffe and Huila, all roasted in Taguig. We name the place, not "local beans".

4Skip the line, keep the ritual

Order ahead, walk in to a cup with your name facing out. Pick up, drive-thru at 5th Ave, or delivery by Grab and foodpanda.

Who we're for, what we say first, and the facts that let us say it. Every claim on this page is something the website already shows.

For people in BGC and Makati who want their coffee right and their mornings easy, GioCoffee is the neighbourhood coffee house that makes every drink to order and writes your name on it, because Philippine beans, a barista who remembers you and a cup waiting on the shelf beat another queue.

Handcrafted coffee house with order-ahead, four stores in BGC and Makati.

Voice & words

We sound like the barista who remembers your order: warm, a little proud of the craft, quick with a joke, never fussy. Short lines, real words, first names, exact times and prices.

Warm

"Your birthday drink is on us."

Talk to one person. Use "you" and their name.

Crafted

"Two ristretto shots and silky whole milk, finished with a tulip of latte art."

Name the ingredient and the step. No adjectives we can't taste.

Neighbourly

"Stay as long as you like, we won't hover."

Plain, friendly, a little local. Taglish where it lands.

Honest

"Spelled right, every time*" *Almost.

Own the small stuff. Footnote the joke, not the price.

Taglish, lightly one local touch per piece, never a whole post

Logo

A round Gio Green badge holding a white take-away cup with a gold band. The steam curls into a G. Next to it, a two-weight wordmark: "Gio" ExtraBold, "Coffee" SemiBold. The files are assets/logo.svg and assets/mark.svg; never redraw them.

How the mark is built 64-unit grid

  1. BadgeA perfect circle in Gio Green: round, open, friendly, like a café door.
  2. RingA thin white ring 4 units in, the rim of a saucer.
  3. CupWhite lid and body on the centre line. It's the take-away cup we hand you.
  4. Gold band + dotThe sleeve, and the dot is where your name goes.
  5. Steam GOne gold stroke that rises from the cup and curls into a G for Gio.
  6. WordmarkPlus Jakarta Sans, tracking −0.03em. "Gio" 800 House Green #14332B, "Coffee" 600 Gio Green #0E6B4E, one word, no space.

Rules

  • Exits are faster than entrances, always
  • Hover effects only where hover exists (hover: hover and pointer: fine)
  • Transitions can be interrupted mid-way; nothing waits for an animation to finish
  • Numbers roll to their new value in 450 ms instead of jumping

  • Sections are quiet by default; each one authors its own moment

Signature moments

The cup assembles

It lands and squashes a touch (0–0.5 s), the sleeve slides up (0.3–0.8 s), the lid presses on (0.6–1 s), the straw drops in on iced cups (0.9–1.4 s). Every time you pick a drink.
